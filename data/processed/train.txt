Given a sequence of discrete tokens x_1, x_2, through x_T, the objective is to maximize
the log-likelihood of observing the correct continuation.
Mathematically, the autoregressive objective decomposes the joint probability:
P(x_1, ..., x_T) = product_{t=1}^T P(x_t | x_1, ..., x_{t-1}).
Each factor is parameterized by deep neural networks with residual connections
and layer normalizations to stabilize gradient propagation across layers.

THEORY: Deep learning models rely on empirical risk minimization.
Given a sequence of discrete tokens x_1, x_2, through x_T, the objective is to maximize
the log-likelihood of observing the correct continuation.
Mathematically, the autoregressive objective decomposes the joint probability:
P(x_1, ..., x_T) = product_{t=1}^T P(x_t | x_1, ..., x_{t-1}).
Each factor is parameterized by deep neural networks with residual connections
and layer normalizations to stabilize gradient propagation across layers.
